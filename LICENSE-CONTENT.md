## What this licence does not cover

- **Code** (`app/`, `data/scripts/`, `tools/`, configuration files) is under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.
- **Data** shipped in `app/public/data/` is derived from third-party datasets and keeps each source's own licence and attribution requirements (CC0, CC BY 3.0/4.0, IODL 2.0, ESA Copernicus terms, swisstopo terms, public domain). Every dataset, its licence and the required attribution text are listed in `research/sources/license-register.md` and in the `meta.json` or `.meta.json` file next to the data. Where this project changed a dataset, the attribution says "(modified)".
